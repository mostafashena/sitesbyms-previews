A studio with roots

Yoga on the Lane was founded in 2012 by Naomi Annand, who came to yoga from a life in dance. The studio sits at 105 Shacklewell Lane, a few minutes' walk from Rectory Road.

Classes centre on vinyasa flow: moving with the breath, at a pace that suits the people in the room. Beginners are welcome, and there are courses and workshops for anyone who wants to go a little deeper.

New to yoga? Start with a beginners' course or an all-levels flow. Just drop us a line and we'll help you pick the right class.

What's on the mat

  • Vinyasa flow

    The heart of the studio: flowing, breath-led classes for all levels.

  • Pregnancy yoga

    Classes for mums-to-be, with time to move, breathe and meet others.

  • Parent & baby

    Bring your little one along; babies are welcome in these classes.

  • Kids yoga

    Playful classes for children, run at the studio.

  • Beginners' courses

    A gentle introduction to the basics over a short course.

  • Workshops & restorative

    Slower, deeper sessions and occasional workshops.
