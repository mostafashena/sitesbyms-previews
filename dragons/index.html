New to yum cha?

“Yum cha” means “drink tea” in Cantonese. It’s a meal for sharing, so bring the family, the team or the whole group.

1

Take a seat, pour the tea

Yum cha runs every lunch, seven days. Weekends get busy, so book ahead.

2

Point at what you like

Trays and trolleys come past your table. Dumplings, buns, roast pork, tarts. Just point.

3

Share everything

Every dish is made for the middle of the table. The price list is on every table.
