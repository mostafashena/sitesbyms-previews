The salon

Small team.
Serious about hair.

K&Co is a neighbourhood salon tucked into the old warehouse streets of Shad Thames. It is the kind of place where you see the same stylist every visit, and they remember what you asked for last time.

The team works with Redken colour and Olaplex bond care, so colour can stay bright and hair can stay healthy, visit after visit.
