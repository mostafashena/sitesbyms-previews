The Dalston salon · Second home of Foster London

Foster London is run by siblings Nadia and Drew Foster. Nadia is an award-winning colourist; Drew spent years styling hair for TV and film before the two of them built a salon of their own in Shoreditch.

In 2018 they opened a second home here on Dalston Lane, a light, bright room a five-minute walk from Dalston Junction Overground.

It is a place for a precise cut, a colour that has been properly thought through, and the kind of friendly chat that makes an hour in the chair go quickly.

Come for a trim, a brave pastel, or a smooth Yuko straightening treatment. Call the salon and the team will find you a time.
