The salon

Adam Alexander London is an eco-conscious salon on Upper Tooting Road, with environmentally friendly products and practices behind every appointment. The specialism is natural-looking hair: balayage and every colour technique, from subtle glosses to full transformations.

Clients come back for years, for colour that grows out beautifully and for a team that listens: to what you want, to your concerns, and to the texture of your hair. Thick, curly, fine or growing back after treatment, every head of hair gets the same care.
