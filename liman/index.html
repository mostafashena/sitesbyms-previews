Our story

Berivan in the dining room,
Niyazi in the kitchen.

Liman is run by Berivan and Niyazi: Niyazi cooks, Berivan looks after the dining room. The menu leans on the charcoal grill, with lamb, chicken and fish cooked over the coals, a table of cold and hot meze, and Mediterranean plates alongside.

“More than just a restaurant, Liman is an orchestra that appeals to your ears and heart.”
