LIMAN.

Liman means “port” in Turkish. Ours is on Penton Street: lamb, chicken and fish over glowing charcoal, a table full of meze, and long evenings.

Berivan in the dining room.
Niyazi at the grill.

Liman is run by Berivan and Niyazi. Niyazi cooks; Berivan looks after every table. The menu leans on the charcoal grill, with cold and hot meze and Mediterranean plates alongside.
