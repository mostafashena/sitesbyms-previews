Our story

From a home kitchen to Kingsland Road.

Ant House began with family recipes cooked at home, and grew into one of the busiest little dining rooms on Kingsland Road, the street London knows for Vietnamese food.

It is relaxed and lively: good for a quick solo lunch, a table of friends or a long dinner with craft beer, house wine and cocktails from the bar. Vegetarian dishes are on the menu too.

What people come back for

Six to start with

  • Bánh mì

    Freshly made baguettes with pork, chicken, or tofu and mushroom. The classic packs five kinds of pork with pâté and coriander.

  • Bun cha

    The Hanoi favourite: grilled pork with noodles and herbs, made fresh.

  • Pho

    Vietnamese noodle soups, a menu staple.

  • Sizzling seafood

    Served on a hot sizzling plate.

  • Honey-roasted chicken

    A house favourite. Glutinous rice with a choice of toppings is on the menu too.

  • Aubergine & tofu

    Com ca tim nuong nam: grilled aubergine and tofu, very tender.

Also on the menu: summer rolls, cheese and prawn spring rolls and fried chicken skin. Prices and the full menu would live here.
