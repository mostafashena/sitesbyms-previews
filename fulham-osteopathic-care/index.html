Meet your osteopath

Isabel has been looking after Fulham for well over a decade

Patients describe Isabel’s “quietly confident way” of finding exactly what the issue is. Some have been coming to her for almost fourteen years, first for pain and then for regular maintenance sessions that keep them moving.

Every appointment starts by listening. Then comes gentle hands-on treatment, a clear explanation of what is going on, and practical advice on exercise and movement that you can take home.
