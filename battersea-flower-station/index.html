Our story

Lisa McCormack, Scott Melville and John Schofield left jobs in marketing, logistics and big chain garden centres to open their own. After years of searching for a site, Battersea Flower Station opened on a cold day in December 2012.

The yard once housed horses and market barrows, and there was a flower shop at the Battersea Park Road entrance just after 1900. Today it's a 4.8-rated local favourite with more than 400 Google reviews.
