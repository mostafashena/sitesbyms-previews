Simple room. Big plates.

Trafalgar Café is the kind of place East Greenwich relies on: a simple, relaxed room where breakfast never stops. Come in after the school run, after a walk by the river, or at two in the afternoon when only a fry-up will do.

Everyone gets looked after here, including the vegetarians and vegans, with their own full breakfasts on the menu.
