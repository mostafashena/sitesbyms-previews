A calm room on a busy road.

Brags & Brams is a café for a quick bite, a long brunch with friends, or a quiet morning with a laptop. Regulars talk about the friendly service, the generous portions, the lovely interior and the fact that nobody rushes you out.

Through the year the café also hosts events, from community get-togethers to a weekly mums' brunch club. There's a second Brags & Brams on Newington Green, too.
