1989Family-run since December

A real family affair.

The Saponara brothers opened their pizzeria and deli on Prebend Street in December 1989, and it has fed the neighbourhood ever since.

The pizzaiolo makes fresh dough every morning, the kitchen rolls pasta daily, and the shelves are stocked with wine, olive oil and pasta brought directly from Italy. Eat in, sit outside when the sun is out, or take a little Italy home.
