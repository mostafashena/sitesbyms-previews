The workshop

Old timepieces deserve slow, careful hands.

Harris Horology is run by horologist and watchmaker James Harris, who started the business to give old watches and clocks the careful handling they need. In 2023 he was awarded FBHI status by the British Horological Institute.

The workshop is open by appointment only. If there’s no trusted repairer near you, watches can also be sent in by post.
