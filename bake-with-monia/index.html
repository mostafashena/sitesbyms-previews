A little Italy, baked daily

Bake with Monia is a bakery and café at 40 Peckham Rye with a deli counter of Italian goods. Come for breakfast, stay for a focaccia sandwich, and on Fridays there's fresh pasta for dinner.

We also make cakes to order and run baking workshops, so you can learn to bake with Monia too.
