Our story

One family, half a century.

Motabir Ali and his family opened Sheba in 1974, when it was one of the very first curry houses on Brick Lane. The street has changed many times since; Sheba is still run by Motabir Ali's family.

We cook Indian, Bangladeshi and Pakistani dishes with fresh, good-quality ingredients, from tandoori grills and seafood to plenty of vegetarian choice. Can't finish? We'll happily pack the rest for you to take home.
