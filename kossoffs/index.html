A family recipe book, reopened.

Wolf Kossoff came to London from Kyiv as a refugee and started baking in the 1920s. The last Kossoffs bakery closed some fifteen years before Aaron, the fourth-generation head baker, opened the doors again at 259 Kentish Town Road.

Some bakes are exactly as Wolf made them, like his apple cake. Next to them sit new ideas: modern viennoiserie, savoury croissants and seasonal specials, with a house blend from Saint Espresso in the cup.
