A neighbourhood table with Andean roots.

El Inca Plebeyo is an Ecuadorian restaurant led by chef Jorge Pacheco. The kitchen cooks the dishes of home: citrus-sharp ceviche, encebollado, meat from the grill and sweet fried plantain.

Plates are designed to share, so order a few and pass them round, with a cocktail, a glass from the wine list or a taste of Ecuadorian sugar-cane spirit.
