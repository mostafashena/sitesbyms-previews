The local chippy

Simple room, serious fish

Nautilus is a family-owned, long-standing favourite on Fortune Green Road. The setting is simple and casual; the attention goes on the fish.

There's a big choice of fish, plus chicken, sausages and a few salads, with beer, house wine and hot or soft drinks.
