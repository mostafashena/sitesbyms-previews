The practice

Fifty years in the same community.

Blue Dental Care is an independent NHS dental surgery on Stockwell Road, a short walk from Brixton and Stockwell stations. Our team of dentists, hygienists and dental nurses sees adults and children for everyday NHS dental care, alongside hygienist appointments and cosmetic treatment.

New NHS patients are welcome when spaces are available. Give us a call and the reception team will tell you what is open right now.
