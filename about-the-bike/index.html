About the Bike is a neighbourhood bike shop and workshop a few minutes from Dalston Kingsland. Come in for a new or refurbished bike, the part you need, or a service on the one you already love.

Customers keep saying the same things: honest advice, fair prices and friendly people who don't try to sell you more than you need.
