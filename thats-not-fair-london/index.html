About the shop

Fashion choices in kids' hands.

That's Not Fair takes classic pieces and makes them kid-friendly: good fabrics, timeless shapes and no shouty patterns, so children can put their own looks together.

Designer labels sit next to our own-brand goodies, and the prices are much kinder than you'd expect. Little ones can take the blue car for a spin while you browse.
