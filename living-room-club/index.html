Camden's living room.

What's cookingJoin a club

Pull up a chair. Stay a while.

The Living Room Club is a relaxed café for working, catching up with friends or sitting outside with a matcha latte. Plant milks come as standard options, the cakes are a reason on their own, and the staff are known for looking after people.

It's also a club: regular language and songwriting groups meet here, so there's usually someone new to talk to.
