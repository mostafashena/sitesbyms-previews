Coached, not crowded

Fit East London is a small gym built around small-group personal training. Each session is led by two experienced coaches and capped at eight people, so everyone gets attention on their form and pace.

Members talk about the coaches, Ben and Mel, and about the people they train alongside: it's a gym where you get to know the room.

Fit East London is also listed as a Wellhub partner gym.

What a session covers

  • 01

  • 03

    High intensity

  • 04

    Time set aside to move well, not just move more.
