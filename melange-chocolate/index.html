Peckham's own chocolate house

Handmade bars and buttons in eighteen flavours, gooey hot chocolate, cakes and ice cream, and on weekend evenings, cocktails.

Melange is run by Isabelle Alaya, the French chocolatier who also founded London's Chocolate Museum. Everything on the counter is made by her: bars, buttons, hot chocolate lollies, and whatever the season calls for.

At Easter that means eggs, rabbits and ducks; at Christmas, penguins and trees. All year round it means a seat in a café where regulars say they feel part of the community the moment they walk in.
