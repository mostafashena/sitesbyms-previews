About the clinic

A calm room on Royal College Street.

NW1 Dental Care offers general, cosmetic and restorative dentistry, with each treatment plan made for the patient in the chair. The team works hard to keep the clinic warm and relaxed, especially for anyone who feels nervous about the dentist.

On site there is 3D CBCT imaging and digital scanning, so planning can happen here rather than at another clinic. Every member of the team is registered with the General Dental Council.
