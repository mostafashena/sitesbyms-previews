Who we are

Sites By MS (M&S Design and Develop Team) designs and builds websites for independent businesses. Contact us at hello@sitesbyms.com or on WhatsApp +44 7405 760048.

About free concepts

Our homepage concepts are built only from information a business has already made public, such as its website, Google profile and listings. Each concept is clearly marked as a concept, is hidden from search engines, and is taken down on request: just reply to our email or write to hello@sitesbyms.com.

What we collect

  • What you send us: your name, business, email, phone, messages and any file you attach.
  • Booking details: the time you choose and how you'd like to talk.
  • For clients: project files, meeting notes, invoices and payment confirmations in your portal.
  • On concept pages: the date and time a concept was opened, so we know it has been seen. We don't use advertising trackers.

How we use it

To reply to you, run calls, deliver your project and keep proper records. We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

Enquiries for up to 12 months. Client project and billing records for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to hello@sitesbyms.com. If you're in the UK, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Project terms

  • Every project starts with a written proposal you approve in your portal.
  • Payments are made by bank transfer, half to start and half before launch. We confirm each payment by hand and you get a receipt.
  • You own your domain, content and photos. The finished website is yours once it's paid in full.
  • Two rounds of changes are included in the review stage. Anything new is quoted first and only charged once you approve it.
