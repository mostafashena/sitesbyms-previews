The neighbourhood

A small neighbourhood ecosystem

Silverberry sits on Pond Street in Hampstead: a relaxed café where the day starts early with coffee and avocado sourdough, carries on through shakshuka and Benedicts, and ends with a slice of burnt Basque cheesecake.

From midday the menu turns to burgers and bowls, and the coffee keeps coming, from classics to chai lattes, matcha and Belgian hot chocolate. It has more than two thousand Google reviews, at 4.6 stars.
