Our story

One chair, one barber. Now four.

Leo Castro, a barber and record collector from Barcelona, opened Castro's in 2019 with his business partner Aisha Khan, a vintage roots singer. It started with one barber and one chair under the arches on Ropewalk.

The specialty is men's hair the way it was worn from the 1930s to the 1960s: Boston cuts, side parts, pompadours and slick backs, alongside modern fades. On Saturdays a vinyl DJ spins records while you wait.

"I like to make it feel like more than a haircut."
Leo Castro, master barber
