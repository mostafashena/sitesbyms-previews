About us

At the edge of Greenwich Market

Monsoon Café sits on Turnpin Lane, right at the edge of Greenwich Market. It's a family-run café with a traditional look: flowers at the entrance, a chequered floor and a few tables out on the pavement.

Come in for a proper coffee and a slice of homemade cake, or stay for a panini, a jacket potato or a cream tea after a morning at the market.
