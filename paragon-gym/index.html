The Paragon family

Built by a world champion.

Stuart Lawson and his brothers opened Paragon in Shoreditch in 1997. Stuart is a two-time kickboxing world title holder, and the energy and commitment that took him there is what he passes on to every student.

The gym is known for being safe, fun and unthreatening. Beginners learn properly from day one, and experienced fighters get a long runway to keep improving with coaches who still walk the walk.

"It brings the nicest people through the door."
