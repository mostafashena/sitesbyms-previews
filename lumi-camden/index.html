Welcome

Light walls, exposed brick and a slightly quirky, luxurious look, with a relaxed crowd that comes for brunch and stays for dessert. Fry-ups and eggs Benedict sit happily next to creole chicken waffles and pistachio pancakes.

Halal chicken and beef options are on the menu, there's a kids' breakfast, and the outdoor seats watch Camden High Street go by.
