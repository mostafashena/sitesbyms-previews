ABOUT US

A neighbourhood tea room

On Royal Hill, Greenwich

Royal Teas is styled as a classic English tea room, with tables inside and out and a homemade vegetarian menu.

Mornings are for breakfast and brunch, lunchtimes for soups and salads, and the afternoon belongs to cream teas and cake.
