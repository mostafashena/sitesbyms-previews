The local

Over twenty years of Greenwich mornings

Black Eye has been pouring coffee on Trafalgar Road since 2004. It's a relaxed, dog-friendly spot with a modern room up front and covered garden seating out the back.

It is the obvious pitstop before or after a run or walk in Greenwich Park, and the courtyard is made for a slow weekend brunch.
