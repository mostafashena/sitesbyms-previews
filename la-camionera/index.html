A bar the community built.

La Camionera started as a fortnightly pop-up in the basement of Off Broadway. When it was time to find a home of its own, the community crowdfunded £100,000, and the bar opened on Well Street three months later.

Today it's a FLINTA-owned lesbian café and wine bar, run by Alex and Clara, open to everyone who wants a good glass and a warm room.
