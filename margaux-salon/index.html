The story

From Lille to North London.

Margaux Cras trained in Lille and opened her first salon there at just twenty. After travelling through Europe, Asia and Oceania, she settled in London and, with Vincent Doucet, opened the first Margaux Salon in Finsbury Park in 2017.

Kentish Town followed in July 2020. The idea hasn't changed: a lighter, more natural approach to hair, the kind that looks like you, only better.

Natural, never overdone.
