01 · The café

A local hangout, not a destination

Time Art Café is small and unhurried. Regulars, freelancers and passers-by come in for coffee taken seriously and a solid selection of teas.

On the walls and shelves: artworks by local and international artists, plus handmade ceramics and jewellery you can take home.
