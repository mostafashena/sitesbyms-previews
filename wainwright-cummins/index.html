I.

The firm

Wainwright & Cummins was founded in 1985 by Andrew Wainwright and Jonathan Cummins. The practice began in Harley Street and moved to Brixton, where it has been based ever since.

One office, one front door, every kind of matter.

Today the firm works from a single office at 57–61 Atlantic Road, having brought its criminal defence and housing team over from Acre Lane so that every client comes through the same door.

Wainwright & Cummins LLP is authorised and regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA number 548743) and holds Conveyancing Quality Scheme accreditation.
