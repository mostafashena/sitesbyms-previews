A Brixton fixture for over thirty years

Patrick Kelly opened Bookmongers on Coldharbour Lane in May 1992, after coming to London from Boston. More than three decades on, the yellow-and-black shopfront is still one of Brixton's best-loved independents.

The stock comes from all over and changes week to week, so regulars drop in often. Ask Patrick if you're looking for something in particular.

“The owner Patrick is friendly and has encyclopaedic knowledge of whatever he's currently stocking.”Time Out
