A little corner of Vietnam on Well Street

Hương is a café for anyone who loves Vietnamese food, vegan or not. Everything on the menu is plant-based, and you can eat in or take away.

Neighbours and visitors have given Hương 4.9 stars on Google. Pop in for a quiet lunch, or come back in the evening on Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays.
