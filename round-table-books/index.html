Books that look like Brixton.

Round Table Books opened in Brixton Village in 2019 as an inclusive bookshop, owned by Black, South Asian and queer founders, with a focus on authors who too often go unseen on the shelves.

In 2026, with the help of a community crowdfunder, the shop moved across the arcade into a bigger space: a bookshop, café, bar and community space, just opposite the old shop.

Founded by Aimée Felone and run with co-directors Jasmina Bidé and Meera Ghanshamdas.
