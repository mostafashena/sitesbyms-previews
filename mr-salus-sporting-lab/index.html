The lab

Naples 2011.
London 2018.
Brixton.

Mr Salus Sporting Lab is part sports clinic, part gait analysis lab, founded by sports therapist Massimo Terracciano.

Massimo opened his first clinic in Naples in 2011, moved to the UK and started the London clinic in 2018. Clients rate the lab 4.9 out of 5 from 176 reviews on Treatwell, and many of them mention Massimo by name for his knowledge and his hands-on approach to necks, shoulders and backs.

In Brixton you’ll find the lab at Studio 7, 360 Coldharbour Lane.
