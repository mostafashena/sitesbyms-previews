The studio

A calm room on one of East London’s busiest roads.

STILL began in Dalston in 2019 as a nail studio built around vegan, cruelty-free products. Today it is a small wellness salon with two treatment rooms and an infrared sauna.

Come in for a manicure, a long pedicure or a quiet session in the sauna, and leave a little slower than you arrived.
