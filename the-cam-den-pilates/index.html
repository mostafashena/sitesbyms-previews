The studio

A calm room, a clear focus and experienced instructors.

The Cam Den is a spacious, quiet studio a few steps from the noise of Camden High Street. Classes on the reformer and on the mat focus on core strength, posture and mobility, taught by experienced instructors.

Core, posture, mobility. Nothing else to think about for the hour.

At the weekend the studio slows right down for crystal sound bath sessions, a chance to lie still and listen.
