The studio

Lydia's studio.

Ceci Studio was founded by Lydia after eight years of teaching Pilates across North London. She wanted a safe, welcoming space that felt completely unintimidating, whether it is your first class or your hundredth.

Every instructor is a current or former professional dancer.

Classes stay small, with just eight reformers in the room. There is a changing room on site.
