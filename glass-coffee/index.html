// the idea

Know where it comes from.

Glass started with one idea: change the way people think about coffee by showing exactly where it comes from and how it's made. It was founded by Carlo, a Brazilian-born entrepreneur, and became known for its hatch in a bright blue shipping container in a Camden market. Today you'll find it at BOXPARK Camden.

The roastery works with separate filter and espresso profiles and loves adventurous processing: thermal shock, anaerobic and co-fermented lots, graded 88 points or more. Ask the team about their favourite producer and be ready for a proper conversation.

Every cup carries a QR code. Scan it to read the story of the farm, the farmer and the community behind your coffee.
