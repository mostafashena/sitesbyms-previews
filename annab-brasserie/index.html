Our story

A family table, opened to Fulham.

Gjulnara learned Lebanese cooking from her husband Fares, and for years cooked his family's recipes at home. When her children urged her to open a restaurant, she found the perfect partner in family friend and experienced restaurateur Bachir Baaklini. Together they opened Annab, named after Gjulnara's favourite Lebanese fruit.

In the kitchen is chef Badih El Asmar, with 15 years of cooking in Lebanon and 22 more in London. Behind the bar, Bassam Eid makes the fresh mint lemonade that guests mention in their reviews, and pours wines from Ksara and Kefraya and four kinds of arak.

"Our vision is to offer warm hospitality and showcase the best traditional Lebanese food."Gjulnara, co-founder

Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free guests are well looked after, with plenty of choice across the menu.
