Come for the meat.
Stay for the people.

Meat People sits in a 1920s Grade II listed building on Essex Road, a couple of minutes from Angel station. The tiled ceiling, wood panelling and steel-framed windows are all part of dinner here.

The kitchen takes its cue from Argentina and Spain: good cuts, cooked on the grill, with chimichurri and small plates alongside.
