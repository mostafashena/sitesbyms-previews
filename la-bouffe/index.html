A family's French heritage.

La Bouffe is run by husband and wife Lawrence and Emily Hartley, who also own Le Petit Citron in Brook Green. Where Le Petit Citron looks to Provence, La Bouffe is inspired by their family's French heritage and the classic Paris bistro.

In the kitchen is head chef Harry, formerly of City Social. Expect generous classics done properly and a warm, neighbourhood welcome.
