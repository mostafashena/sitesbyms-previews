About the centre

Local, independent and here since the Brixton Back Pain Clinic days.

The centre started life as the Brixton Back Pain Clinic and has grown into a team of registered osteopaths, physiotherapists, acupuncturists and massage therapists working side by side.

New patients are welcome. Call the front desk and we'll find the right practitioner and a time that suits you.

Founded by Mardi Jamieson, registered osteopath and practice director, who qualified as an osteopath and naturopath in 1994.Mardi Jamieson · Practice Director
