Our story

Brick Lane's award-winning curry house.

Aladin opened in 1979, cooking for the Bangladeshi community that was making Brick Lane its home.

Along the way, celebrity chef Ainsley Harriott crowned Aladin the best curry house on Brick Lane, the BBC listed it among the world's best curry houses, and HRH Prince Charles praised it on his visit.

"We try to prepare these delicacies in the way they were prepared centuries ago."
