A small, bright café with big windows and a few seats, a short walk from Peckham Rye station.

Nola is run by Josephine and Anthony, who toured the world with the band Oh Wonder before opening their own café on Rye Lane in 2020. It's been a Peckham favourite ever since.

More than 700 Google reviews later, people still come back for the flat white.
