Side A / About

Not tied to one genre. Tied to finding something different.

Hidden Sounds began on Cambridge Heath Road and now lives upstairs in the middle of Ridley Road market, literally hidden from the street.

The idea has stayed the same: no single style, just records that feel different and unique, picked by people who listen to all of them.
