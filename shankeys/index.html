The story

Two cultures, one small room

Shankeys brings together two things that turn out to belong together: the Indian cooking of chef Sacha Henry and the Irish hospitality, and cocktails, of Eoghan Shankey, who learned his trade behind the bar at Milk & Honey.

It started as a pop-up in 2022, did well enough to find a permanent home, and settled into a former bookies on Well Street, a few minutes' walk from Homerton station.

The menu is short and changes often. Expect colourful small plates, something to share, a single pudding and a drinks list where Irish butter and whiskey meet Indian spice.

Eoghan does much of the table work himself, so it feels less like a restaurant and more like being looked after at a very good party.
