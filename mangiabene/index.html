Benvenuti

Mangia Bene means “eat well”, and that is the whole idea. Behind white walls and exposed brick, the open kitchen turns out freshly made pasta, pizzas from the oven and Italian classics, with tables outside when the weather allows. A lovely, unpretentious local, loved for its friendly service and great value.
