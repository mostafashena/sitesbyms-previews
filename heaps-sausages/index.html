Since 1991

Made by Martin

The Simply Sausages story began in 1991 at Smithfield Market, with a shop selling 24 varieties.

Chef Martin Heap is still the man behind the bangers, and Heap's in Greenwich is where you can eat them: in the café, or from the deli counter to cook at home.

It's small inside, with room to perch, and it has the feel of old Greenwich about it.
