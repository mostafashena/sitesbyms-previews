The shop

A storm worth the trip.

Storm in a Teacup opened in July 2011, halfway between Dalston and Shoreditch.

Started by Claudia Raba and stylist Joe Miller, it's home to a huge archive of rare vintage and designer pieces, many found on Claudia's trips around the world. A shop to take your time in.
