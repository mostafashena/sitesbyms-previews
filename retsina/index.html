Our story

A Belsize Park local for over 30 years

Retsina is an award-winning, family-run restaurant that has been part of Belsize Park Village for more than thirty years, and it is still run by the Mina family today.

The room is bright and classic rather than taverna-themed. The cooking is Greek with a modern Mediterranean touch, built on fresh produce, friendly service and good value.
