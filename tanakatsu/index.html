About us

Run by Joe and Koko

Tanakatsu is a neighbourhood katsu restaurant run by Joe and Koko. The menu is short and focused: cutlets breaded in panko and fried until golden, with Japanese curry, rice bowls, teriyaki and a little sushi and sashimi alongside.

Guests keep coming back for the katsu sandwich and the warm, friendly service.
