About the shop

The Hackney Peddler started in 2013 in Hackney Downs Studios, born out of a love for fixing up vintage bikes. Today the shop lives at 89 Stoke Newington Road, with frames on the walls and a workshop out back.

Whether you want a classic steel bike that has already been brought back to life, or you have one at home that needs the same treatment, this is the place to start.
